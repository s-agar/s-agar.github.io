## Five-Year Goal
To be employed as an engineering at a company focused on medical device and/or medical robotics development.
### Steps
- Complete my coursework and graduate from GT.
- Develop projects, join clubs, and conduct research related to medical devices and robotics.
- Network with students, professionals, and almuni.

## Ten-Year Goal
To be leading a project in medical device or medical robotics development at a company or research lab.
### Steps
- Gain experience working at a medical technology company for 5-10 years.
- Complete my master's or PhD in a relevant field if desired.
- Build technical and non-technical skills to improve knowledge and leadership in the field.
