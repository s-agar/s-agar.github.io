These screenshots are from the brainstorming design sprint in which we came up with potential design ideas. They are the ideas we generated that apply those "water bottle" modifications to urine cups. Asking questions and exploring hunches led me to valid ideas, many of which ultimately got implemented into the original solution. Thus, this experience taught me that ideas that seem "bad" or "unappealing" almost never actually are; instead, they are usually worth exploring and often lead to valuable insights. Therefore, this project demonstrates growth in my willingness to explore my curiosity.
